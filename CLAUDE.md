# Project: Joey's Etsy Store Promotion Automation

## Important glossary (per Joey's instructions)
- **"store"** ALWAYS means Joey's Etsy store: **Infinite 4 You**. Whenever Joey says
  "store", "my store", "the store", he means his Etsy shop — never anything else.
  Never ask him to clarify this.

## What this project is
An automation that promotes the store (Etsy shop "Infinite 4 You") by producing
short-form vertical videos (Reels / Shorts / TikToks) and posting content multiple
times daily across:
- TikTok
- Facebook (Reels)
- Instagram (Reels)
- YouTube (Shorts)

## What the store sells
- A series of planner PDF books (digital downloads)
- More digital products will be added over time — content should adapt as the
  catalog grows.

## Skills in this repo (`.claude/skills/`)
- **reel** — generate a complete ready-to-post short-form video package
  (hook, script, on-screen text, per-platform captions, hashtags, CTA)
- **planner** — build the content calendar: what to post, when, on which platform
- **product** — brainstorm and spec new digital products (planner PDFs etc.) for the store
- **listing** — write SEO-optimized Etsy listing copy (title, 13 tags, description)

## Publishing approach (free, no paid tools)
Content is generated here; Joey publishes using the platforms' own free schedulers:
- **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) — schedules Facebook + Instagram Reels
- **YouTube Studio** — schedules Shorts
- **TikTok web uploader** (tiktok.com/upload) — schedules up to 10 days ahead

## Communication notes
- Joey often uses voice dictation; interpret transcription quirks generously.
- Keep replies simple and practical; avoid jargon.
