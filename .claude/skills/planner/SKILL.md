# Content Planner

Create a concrete posting schedule: **which post, on which platform, at what time.**
Default horizon is 7 days unless Joey asks for a different range.

## Defaults (until Joey changes them)

- **Frequency**: 2–3 posts per day per platform (same video can go to all 4 platforms,
  but stagger times so they're not simultaneous).
- **Time slots** (Joey's local time; ask once if unknown, then remember):
  - Morning: 7–9 AM (people planning their day)
  - Midday: 12–1 PM
  - Evening: 7–9 PM (highest engagement; planning tomorrow)
- **Sunday evening** is prime time for planner content ("Sunday reset" audience) — always
  schedule the strongest post of the week there.

## Output format

A simple table per day:

| Time | Platform(s) | Post (reel title) | Angle |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-------|

Followed by a short "batch day" note: which videos to record/assemble in one sitting.

## Planning rules

- Rotate content angles (see the `reel` skill) — never two of the same angle back-to-back on one platform.
- Each video posts to all 4 platforms but on a staggered schedule (e.g. TikTok first,
  IG +2h, YouTube +4h, Facebook +6h).
- Weight themes to the calendar: back-to-school (Aug–Sep), new year planning (Dec–Jan),
  Sunday resets, Monday motivation, month-end "plan next month" pushes.
- When a new product launches in the store, front-load 3–5 launch posts in the first week.
- End every plan with the free-scheduler workflow reminder:
  1. Batch-create videos (CapCut, free)
  2. Schedule FB + IG in Meta Business Suite (free)
  3. Schedule Shorts in YouTube Studio (free)
  4. Schedule TikToks at tiktok.com/upload (free, up to 10 days ahead)
